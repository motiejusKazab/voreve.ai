# Voreve.ai brief (self-authored under explicit creative delegation)

Written in the scroll-craft format. The user did not interview; these are authored decisions, not quotes.

**Product:** Voreve is an AI call assistant. The phone is the storytelling device; the AI is the product.
**Next action (one label everywhere):** Book a Demo. Secondary: Get in Touch (email).
**Must believe by the end:** "This is an AI employee for phone calls, and two students are building it."

## Grammar
Live surface + chaptered story. One fixed 3D phone is the "working surface"; scroll is the call's timeline.
Device families used, never twice in a row: `pin`/scrub (call story), `parallax` (phone travel), `reveal` (line masks),
pointer/drag devices (day scrubber, founders line, answer handset), `kinetic` (word-by-word text).

## Feeling curve
| Act | Feeling | What causes it |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | Anticipation | A phone rings, slightly buzzing, in the dark |
| The call | Relief | Swipe to answer: Ringing becomes Connected |
| Listen | Intimacy | Live waveform; words type in as you scroll |
| Understand | Clarity | Transcript folds into an intent card; "Still needed" pulses |
| Act | Satisfaction | Steps resolve into a confirmed booking |
| Result (PEAK) | Calm resolve | The phone settles at centre; "One call handled." |
| Why Voreve | Confidence | A day of calls falls onto the timeline; drag the hour |
| Founders | Warmth | The phone turns landscape and shows two names with one live line between them |
| Final CTA | Invitation | Your own call is ringing, with the phone beside you: slide the handset and it answers too |

## The peak
"The phone swung to the middle, the screen said Call complete, and the page said *One call handled.*"
It has the largest scroll span (150svh) and the quiet act (Act) before it.

## Tell-someone sentence
"It's the site where you scroll through one phone call and watch an AI answer it, and at the end your own call is ringing."

## Signature move
The scroll-scrubbed phone whose screen morphs between call states (incoming, listening, intent, action, result).
The ending reuses the same "slide to answer" gesture for booking.

## Honesty rules kept
No invented customers, metrics, awards, funding, photos or quotes. The example call is labelled illustrative everywhere.
Booking URL, email address and form endpoint are left unset in `config.js` and show honest "not connected yet" states.

## Not verified
Real-device touch scrolling and frame rate (headless Chrome only).

## Decisions after review
- One dark theme only (no paper or orange sections).
- Removed "What a call can lead to": no clear purpose, and it implied capabilities (Route, Handle) that are not verified.
- The phone returns beside the contact section and reacts to the visitor answering.
- The phone no longer disappears after the value section: it turns landscape to show the founders, then returns upright to the contact section.
